In nineteen-oh-two, the French paid the people of Hanoi for every rat they killed. And ended up with more rats.
The city had just built brand new sewers, the pride of the colony.
The rats loved them. A warm, dark highway, running under every fancy house.
So the government hired rat catchers. Twenty thousand rats a day! It still wasn't enough.
New plan! One cent, for every rat's tail!
And the tails pour in. Thousands of them. The officials are thrilled.
Then somebody notices something odd on the street. Rats. Alive. With no tails.
You see, a dead rat pays you once. But a rat with no tail goes home, and makes more rats. With more tails.
Then the inspectors find something worse, just outside town. It's not a rat farm! It was a rat farm.
People were breeding rats, just to sell the tails.
So the French cancelled the reward. They had tried to buy fewer rats. And paid for more.
